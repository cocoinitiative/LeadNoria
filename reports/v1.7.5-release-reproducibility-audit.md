## 4. REPRODUCIBILITY & ARCHIVE DETERMINISM ANALYSIS

A controlled reproducibility experiment was performed by building into isolated temporary directories (`temp-repro/build-A` and `temp-repro/build-B`):

1. **Compilation Determinism:**
   - The compiled JavaScript bundles (`app.js`, `content-script.js`, `service-worker.js`), `manifest.json`, and `styles.css` compiled by esbuild are **100% deterministic**. Rebuilding multiple times produced identical SHA-256 hashes for all bundled source files.
2. **ZIP Archive Timestamp Variation (Environmental Limitation):**
   - The `archiver` library embeds the file modification timestamps (`mtime`) in MS-DOS format into the PKZIP Local and Central Directory headers.
   - When builds are executed at different times (e.g. 2 seconds apart), the resulting ZIP checksum differs (`DA1C6092...` vs `9F02FAD6...`), even though the uncompressed contents are identical byte-for-byte.
   - **Single-Run Determinism:** In [scripts/build-extension.mjs](file:///e:/project%20anti/leadnoria/scripts/build-extension.mjs), both `dist/leadnoria-v1.7.5.zip` and `extension.zip` are generated in immediate succession from the exact same directory without touching the files, ensuring they share identical timestamps and are 100% byte-for-byte identical.
