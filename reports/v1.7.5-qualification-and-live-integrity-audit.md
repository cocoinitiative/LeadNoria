## 2. DEFECT INVESTIGATION & AUTOMATIC REPAIR

### Defect 2.1: False-Positive Qualification of Non-Furniture Advertiser (`Psychic Sofa`)
- **Investigation:** In Meta Ad Library ID `1130123122204872`, advertiser `Psychic Sofa` advertised mobile psychic telephone reading and tarot card sessions with CTA "Install Now" and offer copy "First 10 mins free". The business has no furniture showroom, factory, or catalog.
- **Root Cause:**
  1. `NEGATIVE_CATEGORIES` lacked spiritual/astrology/psychic and mobile digital media verticals.
  2. Isolated product keywords in entity names (`sofa`) previously triggered `STRONG` entity identity, allowing advertisers with commercial CTAs to satisfy Tier 2 without vertical corroboration.
- **Implementation Fix:**
  - Added `astrology_psychic_spiritual` and `digital_entertainment_media` negative categories to both [relevanceEngine.ts](file:///e:/project%20anti/leadnoria/src/extension/relevanceEngine.ts#L330-L375) and [evidenceWaterfall.ts](file:///e:/project%20anti/leadnoria/src/extension/evidenceWaterfall.ts#L35-L65).
  - Differentiated root vertical descriptors (`furniture`, `furnishing`, `woodwork`, `interior decor`) from isolated product nouns (`sofa`, `chair`, `desk`). Product nouns alone yield `MODERATE` entity identity (`SIGNAL_ENTITY_NAME_PRODUCT`).
  - Tier 4 and Criterion 1 now strictly require actual category/catalog corroboration; a product term in name + commercial CTA alone remains `UNCERTAIN`.
  - Disqualified candidates with hard contradictions receive reason code `REJECT_CONTRADICTION_IDENTITY`.

### Defect 2.2: Accessory-Only and Slipcover False Positives
- **Investigation:** Advertisers such as "Sofa Cover Tex Turkish" selling elastic slipcovers and dustproof furniture covers were qualifying as furniture manufacturers because ad copy mentioned target furniture words ("couches", "furniture").
- **Root Cause:** `isAccessoryOnly` contained `&& !strongCategory`, which was negated as soon as ad copy mentioned 2 covered furniture items.
- **Implementation Fix:**
  - Added `ACCESSORY_ONLY_INDICATORS` (`sofa cover`, `slipcover`, `cushion cover`, `furniture protector`, `chair cover`).
  - Governed accessory detection by `!hasRootVerticalEntity` (i.e., businesses lacking core manufacturing/showroom root identity are classified as `UNCERTAIN` with `UNCERTAIN_ACCESSORY_ONLY`).

### Defect 2.3: `activeAdCount` Metric Invariant Hardening
- **Investigation:** If an advertiser had only inactive cards or merged cards where some were inactive, fallbacks previously defaulted to `adLibraryIds.length`, falsely reporting inactive ads as active.
- **Root Cause:**
  - In [metaAdapter.ts](file:///e:/project%20anti/leadnoria/src/extension/metaAdapter.ts), `activeAdCount` had a fallback to `activeIds.length || totalAdCount`.
  - In [entityResolver.ts](file:///e:/project%20anti/leadnoria/src/extension/entityResolver.ts), merging an inactive card did not preserve pre-existing active IDs if `activeAdLibraryIds` array had not yet been materialized on the target lead.
- **Implementation Fix:**
  - `activeAdCount` is strictly defined as `activeAdLibraryIds.length` (0 when all ads are inactive).
  - Pre-existing target state initializes `activeAdLibraryIds` deterministically from `target.adLibraryIds.slice(0, target.activeAdCount)` before merging candidates.

### Defect 2.4: Multi-Locale Ad Library ID Extraction Boundaries
- **Investigation:** When extracting IDs from international Ad Library pages (e.g., Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese), date strings adjacent to the ID (`11 Feb 2025`, `26 Mars 2025`, `19 Ago 2025`) risked digit leakage.
- **Implementation Fix:**
  - Expanded `monthMatch` regex in [metaAdapter.ts](file:///e:/project%20anti/leadnoria/src/extension/metaAdapter.ts#L450-L480) to cover: `Jan|Feb|Mar|Apr|May|Jun|Jul|Aug|Sep|Oct|Nov|Dec|Ene|Abr|Ago|Dic|Janv|Févr|Mars|Avr|Mai|Juin|Juil|Août|Sept|Mär|Okt|Dez|Fev|Set|Out|Gen|Mag|Giu|Lug|Ott`.
